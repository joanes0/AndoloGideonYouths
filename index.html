Who We Are

Andolo Gideon Youths is a grassroots movement born in Langata, Andolo. We unlock potential through skill-building, environmental action, and community-led solutions.

🌟 Our Vision

A self-reliant generation of young leaders transforming Langata, Andolo and beyond.

🎯 Our Mission
